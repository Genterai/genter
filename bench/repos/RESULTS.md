Written by `node bench/repos/run.mjs` on 2026-10-20 with genter-cli 0.3.1 and the readers adr, changelog, decisions, eip, kep, nep, pep, pip, proposals, rfc, rfd, banners, obsidian (those of decision records: adr, decisions, eip, kep, nep, pep, pip, proposals, rfc, rfd). The method is in [README.md](README.md); the numbers are in [results/2026-10-20-heldout.json](results/2026-10-20-heldout.json).

## The gate, on the 46 held out

**Does not pass.** The gate (step 1.10 of Genter's open-source plan) passes when the median repository has 5 or more decision records and 15 of the 50 have an explicit replaced mark. It is judged on the held-out sample ([heldout.json](heldout.json), picked on 2026-10-20): the same rule and groups as the first 50 (candidates.json), over repositories not among them, picked and committed before the readers ran on any of them. The first 50 are not a fair test any more: the readers were widened looking at them (below). The held-out sample holds 46 repositories, not 50: only 14 of the 18 decision archives the rule takes passed the check outside the first 50 (candidates.json lists every one named, and those left out as not of the kind). The thresholds are applied as written: the median of the 46, and 15 repositories with a mark.

* The median repository has **0** decision records the readers read (below 5); 9 of the 46 have 5 or more read, and the median needs half of them to. By each group's own pattern for its records (candidates.json), whatever their format, the median repository holds 12.5 (the changelog group holds none by definition).
* **20 of 46** have at least one document that carries a mark the readers read (at least 15); in 6 of them a mark names a replacing document that is in the repository (where Genter puts the current answer first).
* The gate counts a mark of any kind, from any reader, the fallback ones (banners, front matter) included, as the readers read it: a heading or a sentence a fallback reader takes for a document's own mark counts too. By kind, the repositories with a mark: superseded 7, moved 6, deprecated 13, obsolete 5; with a superseded or moved mark (one that names a replacement or a new place) 10, below 15; with a superseded mark alone 7, below 15.

## Per repository

Decision records: documents a reader of decision records (its manifest says `records: true`) reads a date, a status or a mark in, and entries of a decision log; in the tree: the files that match the group's own pattern for the repository's records (candidates.json), read or not. Replaced: documents that carry a mark the readers read (superseded, deprecated, obsolete, archived, moved, removed, unmaintained), from any reader; named: the mark names what replaces it; there: the replacing document it links, or its id names, is in the repository. Dated: documents a reader reads a date in. Changelog deprecations: entries' deprecated and removed items, not marks of a document.
